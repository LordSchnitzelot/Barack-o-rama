#   masculine (with nikkud) | other forms, separated by commas

# - A form in brackets is its spelling without nikkud, when it differs from just removing the
#   nikkud: מְזֻיֶּפֶת (מזויפת). A form with no brackets counts as itself without nikkud.
# - A word with no nikkud is an extra accepted spelling: טיפש.
# - Most feminine, feminine plural and extra spellings are the owner's; the masculine plurals, and
#   lines marked "# Claude", were written by Claude and are listed for a check.
