# English-for-Hebrew-speakers dictionary: the meanings and translations, one per line.
# tools/build-dictionary.py turns this file into dictionary-en.json and dictionary-he.json,
# taking nikkud, inflected forms and frequency ranks from Wiktionary and OpenSubtitles.
#
#   english | part of speech | hebrew | label
#
# - Part of speech: n (noun), v (verb), adj, adv, pron, prep, conj, det, num, q (question word),
#   modal, intj (interjection or set phrase).
# - Hebrew is written without nikkud; the build script finds the nikkud in Wiktionary.
#   Verbs: past / infinitive ("הלך / ללכת"). Extra accepted forms after commas ("אתה, את").
#   Hebrew synonyms in one meaning group with "+" ("כועס + עצבני"). Adjective forms that Wiktionary
#   lacks go in forms.txt.
# - Label: only when the English word has more than one meaning in this file, or to clarify.
#   Words with the same Hebrew are grouped together as synonyms.
# - Phrases with a meaning of their own ("give up", "how are you") are entries too (spec 3.3).
